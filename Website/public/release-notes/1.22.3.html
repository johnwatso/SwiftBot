SwiftBot 1.22.3

Version 1.22.3 · Build 2026073111

A reliability and Sweep update: Patchy now finds the correct AMD driver, music lookups return promptly, and Sweep makes weekly voice recognition easier to manage.

🛠️Patchy and Music Reliability

Driver and music lookups now avoid the stale or failing paths that could hold up results.

  • Correct latest AMD driver: Patchy now recognises standard three-part Adrenalin releases and ignores specialised AMD pages that could incorrectly outrank the public driver.
  • Faster music search: Music lookups no longer wait on the failed song.link enrichment request. Apple catalogue results return directly, with search links still available for Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, and YouTube.

🏆Weekly Voice MVP

Sweep can now celebrate the most active voice member on a schedule you choose.

  • Scheduled MVP announcement: Enable a Weekly Voice MVP on any Sweep rule, choose its weekday, local hour, and message template, and SwiftBot will post the rolling seven-day voice leader to that rule’s channel.
  • Safe, once-per-week delivery: The announcement is sent only by the Primary bot, mentions the winner safely, and records the completed week to prevent duplicates.
  • Send Test MVP: Post a real preview immediately to verify the channel, mention, and wording. A test does not consume the scheduled weekly announcement.

✨Clearer Sweep Suggestions

Suggestions are easier to evaluate without losing the details.

  • Focused recommendations: Sweep surfaces up to three high-confidence recommendations first, with no more than one best match per channel.
  • More when needed: Lower-priority and alternative strategies stay available in an expandable list instead of filling the page.
  • Cleaner decisions: Applying a suggestion clears its competing suggestions for that channel.

🚢Release Details

Version and regression coverage were refreshed for this release.

  • Updated MARKETING_VERSION to 1.22.3 and CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION to 2026073111.
  • Added regression tests for AMD release selection and weekly MVP formatting and scheduling.
