SwiftBot 1.22.4

Version 1.22.4 · Build 2026073115

A focused quality update for music sharing and the weekly Sweep Voice MVP.

🎧Channel-Scoped Music Links

Make a dedicated music channel helpful without making the rest of the server noisy.

  • Choose where it runs: Use the gear beside /music in the Mac app, or Commands in the Web UI, to enable the watcher and select one or more text channels.
  • Useful cross-platform replies: Supported Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, YouTube Music, and SoundCloud track links produce Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, and YouTube listening links.
  • Quiet by design: The watcher is off by default, ignores playlists and bot messages, and only replies in selected channels.

🏆One Weekly Voice MVP Card

The weekly voice recognition stays visible without filling the channel with repeats.

  • Always current: Sweep now updates one pinned MVP card in place for each policy instead of adding a new leaderboard card each week.
  • Winner notification: The new MVP still receives a fresh Discord mention, while the pinned card remains tidy and protected from normal Sweep cleanup.
  • Resilient recovery: If the pinned card is deleted, SwiftBot creates and pins a replacement at the next scheduled announcement.
