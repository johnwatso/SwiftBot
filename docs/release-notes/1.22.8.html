SwiftBot 1.22.8

Version 1.22.8 · Build 2026081816

This Announcer reliability update gives operators a dependable quiet switch, makes an empty room a deliberate exit, and keeps spoken messages natural during solo conversations.

⏸️Announcer Controls That Stick

A deliberate disconnect should not be undone by an automatic recovery a few moments later.

  • /announce disconnect: leaves the caller’s configured Announcer channel and pauses automatic startup, auto-join, stream-join, and recovery for one hour.
  • Resume on demand: /announce join, /announce rejoin, and the Announcer reconnect control clear the hold immediately.
  • Empty room behaviour: Until last person leaves now pauses reads immediately, waits a configurable 5–120 seconds (30 seconds by default), then leaves if nobody returns.

🛡️Quieter, More Deliberate Recovery

SwiftBot now distinguishes a recoverable blip from a voice session that needs time to settle.

  • Recovery circuit breaker: after its bounded retry budget is exhausted, the Announcer waits five minutes instead of cycling through new automatic joins.
  • Real media-path health checks: the watchdog verifies media components, UDP keepalives, and active audio delivery—not merely that a WebSocket remains open.
  • Natural solo reads: the first message identifies its author, then repeated messages from the same person omit the name for two minutes. A different speaker or a quiet gap restores attribution.

🩺Better Evidence When Something Does Go Wrong

The Announcer UI and exported diagnostics now explain why it is quiet, recovering, or intentionally waiting.

  • Exports include the manual-hold state and the recovery circuit breaker’s reason, retry count, and timing.
  • The Announcer status view shows an active manual hold or recovery cool-off rather than looking silently disconnected.
